2. Product Overview
NeoBank is a retail internet banking portal for customer account management, beneficiary management, simulated transfers, scheduled transfers, statements and loans, supplemented by AI-powered fraud detection, spending insights, loan pre-assessment, KYC checking and banking assistance.
The product is intentionally product-focused. Engineering implementation details are out of scope for this PRD and belong in SRS/HLD/LLD/technical design documentation.

3. Product Vision
Product Vision: NeoBank aims to provide a secure, intuitive and intelligent digital banking experience where customers can manage accounts, transfers and loans while receiving AI-powered insights and assistance.
The vision does not introduce any capability beyond the approved BRD; AI remains an assisting capability and not an autonomous financial actor.
4. Product Goals
Make account visibility easy and understandable.
Provide a straightforward beneficiary-management journey with lifecycle controls.
Make simulated fund transfers clear and confirmable through review and OTP.
Support future-dated and recurring simulated transfers.
Make statements easy to generate, view and download.
Simplify loan application and EMI estimation while preserving human decision ownership.
Protect sessions through the confirmed five-minute idle timeout and specified password interaction.
Provide authorized branch-level approval workflows.
Provide useful AI-assisted fraud, spending, loan, KYC and read-only assistant experiences.
Priority Rule: Individual feature priorities are not defined in the BRD. The PRD therefore uses 'TBD – Product Owner Decision Required' rather than inventing priorities.

BNK-FR-01 – Account Summary
Module: Accounts
BRD Traceability: BNK-FR-01
TR Traceability: TR-03
Requirement Classification: Confirmed Requirement – translated from the approved BRD.
Priority: TBD – Product Owner Decision Required
Release: TBD – Product Owner Decision Required
Feature Objective
Enable an authenticated customer to quickly understand their available banking relationships and open account-level information without losing account context.
User Problem
Customers need a consolidated view of Savings, Current and FD accounts, with clear balances and account details.
Product Behavior
The product presents authorized accounts, distinguishes supported account types, shows balances and status, and allows the customer to select an account and view account information in tabs. Account number presentation must appropriately protect sensitive information.
Entry Point
Authenticated Dashboard → Accounts
User / Roles
Customer. Staff access outside explicitly approved permissions remains TBD.
Screen / UI Requirements
Accounts page with an account list/cards area.
Account type visibly identified for Savings, Current and FD.
Balance shown prominently for each accessible account.
Account selection preserves selected-account context.
Account Details area uses tabs for account-specific information.
FD-specific information appears only when relevant.
Account status and masked account number are visible where applicable.
User Actions
Open Accounts.
Select an account.
Navigate account-detail tabs.
Refresh account information where supported.
Data Display Requirements
Account type
Balance
Account status
Masked account number
FD-specific information where relevant
Validation
User must have permission to view the account.
Returned account context must belong to/resolve to the authorized user.
Sensitive account numbers must be appropriately masked.
Loading State
Show an Accounts loading state until account information is available.
Empty State
If no accounts are available, show a clear empty state without implying an account exists.
Error State
If retrieval fails, show a recoverable error state and do not expose unauthorized or stale-sensitive information as though it were current.
Success State
Authorized account summaries and selected account details are displayed accurately.
Confirmation
No business confirmation is required for viewing account information.
Notifications
In-app error/empty-state messaging where applicable. Other channels are TBD.
Permissions
Customer: View. Staff permissions: TBD.
Business Rules
Only authorized account information is presented.
Supported account types are Savings, Current and FD.
Available-balance semantics, if different from balance, are TBD.
No additional account fields are treated as confirmed unless defined by the BRD.
Dependencies
Authentication; account data.
Audit
Account viewing and material account activity should be auditable; exact audit events/retention are TBD.
Acceptance Criteria
AC-01-01: Given an authenticated customer with an accessible account, when Accounts is opened, then the account summary is displayed.
AC-01-02: Given a selected account, when details are opened, then account-specific information is presented in tabs.
AC-01-03: Given account data is unavailable, then the product shows an appropriate error/empty state without unauthorized data.
Open Questions
What additional account-detail fields are required?
What is the exact masking standard?
Should available balance be shown separately from account balance?

BRD Traceability: BNK-FR-02
TR Traceability: TR-01 L3
Requirement Classification: Confirmed Requirement – translated from the approved BRD.
Priority: TBD – Product Owner Decision Required

Feature Objective
Provide a controlled beneficiary lifecycle so customers can add valid destinations and use them for transfers only after activation.
User Problem
Customers need a reliable way to create transfer beneficiaries while reducing duplicate or premature use.
Product Behavior
Customers can view beneficiaries, add a beneficiary, use IFSC lookup/autosuggest, validate beneficiary information, and see pending/active/failed activation states. A beneficiary remains unavailable for transfer until the activation condition is satisfied.
Entry Point
Dashboard → Beneficiaries → Add Beneficiary
User / Roles
Customer.
Screen / UI Requirements
Beneficiary list with lifecycle status.
Add Beneficiary form.
IFSC lookup/autosuggest interaction.
Visible pending/active/failed status.
Clear transfer restriction messaging for inactive beneficiaries.
User Actions
View beneficiaries.
Enter beneficiary name, account number, confirmation account number, bank/IFSC information.
Use IFSC lookup/autosuggest.
Submit beneficiary.
Review lifecycle status.
Use activated beneficiary in Fund Transfer.
Data Display Requirements
Beneficiary name
Account number
Confirm account number
Bank name where available
IFSC
Beneficiary status
Validation
Account number confirmation must match.
IFSC must pass the configured validation/lookup.
Mandatory information must be complete.
Duplicate beneficiary conditions must be checked.
Loading State
Show lookup/validation progress without allowing duplicate submission.
Empty State
If no beneficiaries exist, show an empty state with an Add Beneficiary action.
Error State
Show field-level validation and service failure states; do not create unintended active beneficiaries.
Success State
Beneficiary is created in the correct lifecycle state and its status is visible.
Confirmation
Submission may require security confirmation per approved business rule; exact requirement is TBD.
Notifications
Beneficiary created/pending/activated notifications; channel beyond in-app is TBD.
Permissions
Customer: View/Create; Edit/Delete details are subject to lifecycle rules and TBD policy.
Business Rules
A valid new beneficiary is placed into pending activation.
Cooling-period duration is TBD – Business Decision Required.
Beneficiary cannot be used for transfer before activation.
Duplicate handling must prevent unintended duplicate destinations.
Modification/deletion behavior depends on business decision and beneficiary status.
Dependencies
Authentication; IFSC lookup; notification capability; Fund Transfer.
Audit
Creation, activation, status change and material modification/deletion should be auditable; retention TBD.
Acceptance Criteria
AC-02-01: Given valid beneficiary information, when submitted, then a pending beneficiary is created.
AC-02-02: Given a beneficiary still within the cooling period, when used for transfer, then the transfer is blocked.
AC-02-03: Given the activation condition is satisfied, then the beneficiary becomes Active.
Open Questions

22. Product Security Requirements
Authentication is required for customer banking functionality.
Authorization must follow role permissions.
Five-minute inactivity timeout is a confirmed product requirement.
Transfer confirmation and OTP are explicit product controls.
Sensitive account information, password input and KYC information must be handled and displayed appropriately.
High-risk transactions require additional confirmation.
AI must not independently execute financial transactions.
Banking Assistant must remain read-only.
Expired sessions cannot perform protected actions.
Scope Boundary: This section defines product behavior only. Authentication standards, cryptographic details, token architecture and infrastructure controls belong in technical security documentation.

26. Product Acceptance Criteria
Feature-level acceptance criteria are defined inside each feature specification. The consolidated list below provides a traceable view for Product, QA and UAT.
BNK-FR-01
AC-BNK-FR-01-F01: Given an authenticated customer with an accessible account, when Accounts is opened, then the account summary is displayed.
AC-BNK-FR-01-F02: Given a selected account, when details are opened, then account-specific information is presented in tabs.
AC-BNK-FR-01-F03: Given account data is unavailable, then the product shows an appropriate error/empty state without unauthorized data.
BNK-FR-02
AC-BNK-FR-02-F01: Given valid beneficiary information, when submitted, then a pending beneficiary is created.
AC-BNK-FR-02-F02: Given a beneficiary still within the cooling period, when used for transfer, then the transfer is blocked.
AC-BNK-FR-02-F03: Given the activation condition is satisfied, then the beneficiary becomes Active.
BNK-FR-03
AC-BNK-FR-03-F01: Given valid transfer information, when the customer confirms, then OTP is requested.
AC-BNK-FR-03-F02: Given valid OTP and no blocking risk rule, then the simulated transfer completes and a reference is displayed.
AC-BNK-FR-03-F03: Given invalid/expired OTP, then the transfer does not complete.
AC-BNK-FR-03-F04: Given a high-risk assessment, then the transaction is held for additional confirmation.
BNK-FR-04
AC-BNK-FR-04-F01: Given a valid future date, then a future-dated schedule can be created.
AC-BNK-FR-04-F02: Given a valid recurring configuration, then a recurring schedule can be created.
AC-BNK-FR-04-F03: Given a prohibited past date, then schedule creation is prevented.
BNK-FR-05
AC-BNK-FR-05-F01: Given a valid account/date range, then a statement is generated.
AC-BNK-FR-05-F02: Given a generated statement, then it opens in a new tab.
AC-BNK-FR-05-F03: Given a generated statement, then PDF download is available.
AC-BNK-FR-05-F04: Given no transactions, then an appropriate empty result is shown.
BNK-FR-06
AC-BNK-FR-06-F01: Given valid loan application data, then the application can be submitted.
AC-BNK-FR-06-F02: Given slider changes, then the EMI result updates according to calculator behavior.
AC-BNK-FR-06-F03: Given AI pre-assessment output, then it is labeled advisory.
AC-BNK-FR-06-F04: Given a submitted application, the Loan Officer can make the final decision.
BNK-FR-07
AC-BNK-FR-07-F01: Given five minutes of inactivity, then the timeout popup is displayed.
AC-BNK-FR-07-F02: Given an expired session, then protected access requires authentication.
AC-BNK-FR-07-F03: Given password entry, then the virtual keyboard is available.
BNK-FR-08
AC-BNK-FR-08-F01: Given an authorized Branch Admin and a pending record, then the record can be reviewed and actioned.
AC-BNK-FR-08-F02: Given approval, then status changes to approved.
AC-BNK-FR-08-F03: Given rejection and required reason, then status changes to rejected.
AC-BNK-FR-08-F04: Given unauthorized access, then the admin area is inaccessible.
BNK-AI-01
AC-BNK-AI-01-A01: Given an eligible simulated transaction, when fraud assessment is available, then a fraud-risk assessment is produced.
AC-BNK-AI-01-A02: Given a high-risk assessment, then the transaction is held and additional confirmation is required.
AC-BNK-AI-01-A03: AI never directly executes a transfer.
BNK-AI-02
AC-BNK-AI-02-A01: Given eligible transactions, then categories are assigned where possible.
AC-BNK-AI-02-A02: Monthly category totals are presented.
AC-BNK-AI-02-A03: The explanation is grounded in available transaction data.
BNK-AI-03
AC-BNK-AI-03-A01: Given sufficient loan data, then AI returns an advisory pre-assessment.
AC-BNK-AI-03-A02: Suggested amount/reasons are labeled advisory.
AC-BNK-AI-03-A03: Loan Officer remains final decision maker.
AC-BNK-AI-03-A04: AI cannot independently approve or reject.
BNK-AI-04
AC-BNK-AI-04-A01: Given an accepted upload, name/DOB/ID extraction is attempted.
AC-BNK-AI-04-A02: Extracted values are compared with customer records.
AC-BNK-AI-04-A03: Mismatches are flagged.
AC-BNK-AI-04-A04: Failed/poor-quality extraction does not fabricate values.
BNK-AI-05
AC-BNK-AI-05-A01: Supported balance queries return authorized data.
AC-BNK-AI-05-A02: Supported last-transaction queries return authorized data.
AC-BNK-AI-05-A03: Supported FD-rate queries return configured data.
AC-BNK-AI-05-A04: Transfer instructions are refused and never executed, initiated, approved or confirmed.
AC-BNK-AI-05-A05: Unsupported/unreliable questions do not produce fabricated answers.

37. Final PRD Summary
Product Summary
NeoBank is a retail internet banking portal covering account visibility, beneficiary lifecycle, simulated fund transfers, scheduled transfers, statements, loans, security controls, branch approvals and five AI-powered product capabilities.
Features Covered
BNK-FR-01 – Account Summary, BNK-FR-02 – Add Beneficiary and Activation, BNK-FR-03 – Simulated Fund Transfers, BNK-FR-04 – Future-Dated and Recurring Transfers, BNK-FR-05 – Generate and Download Statements, BNK-FR-06 – Loan Application and EMI Calculator, BNK-FR-07 – Session Timeout and Virtual Keyboard, BNK-FR-08 – Account and Loan Approval, BNK-AI-01 – Transaction Fraud Risk Assessment, BNK-AI-02 – Transaction Categorization and Monthly Spending Explanation, BNK-AI-03 – AI Loan Eligibility Pre-Assessment, BNK-AI-04 – KYC Information Extraction and Mismatch Detection, BNK-AI-05 – Read-Only Banking Assistant.
User Roles Covered
Customer, Teller, Loan Officer and Branch Admin. Staff permissions not explicitly defined in the BRD remain TBD.
AI Capabilities
Fraud Detection, Spend Insights, Loan Eligibility Pre-Assessment, KYC Document Check and Banking Assistant.
MVP / Release Status
MVP scope, feature priorities and release assignments are Product Owner decisions because the approved BRD does not explicitly define them.
Major Dependencies
Authentication, IFSC lookup, OTP, account/transaction data, PDF generation, loan data, EMI calculator, KYC processing, AI services, fraud scoring, FD-rate source and Branch Admin authentication.
Major Risks
AI false positives/negatives, incorrect AI categorization or extraction, loan recommendation misunderstanding, OTP/transfer failure, AI availability, privacy concerns and banking-assistant accuracy.
Open Product Decisions
Cooling period, transfer limits, OTP rules, recurrence options, loan criteria/limits/rates, fraud thresholds/fallbacks, KYC document types/confidence/retention, staff permissions, notification channels, FD-rate source, Branch Admin authentication and Assistant fallback/low-confidence behavior.
Critical Product Safety: All money movement is simulated. AI cannot independently transfer money. Banking Assistant is read-only. AI loan eligibility is advisory only; Loan Officer owns the final decision.
